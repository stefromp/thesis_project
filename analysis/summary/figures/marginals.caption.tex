Marginal distributions of two numerical columns of Beijing (pm2.5, DEWP) under each rule. Top: density of the real training data (grey) and of the synthetic data, pooled over the 20 sampled tables (lines); bottom: synthetic minus real density per bin, where the differences behind the Shape error become visible. Bins: 50 equal-width bins between the real 0.5\% and 99.5\% quantiles; both densities are counts divided by (all rows $\times$ bin width), so mass outside the window lowers the curve instead of being renormalised. The percentage after each rule in the legend is that rule's Shape error of the column ($100\,(1-\text{KS complement})$, mean over the 20 tables). The x axis of pm2.5 is logarithmic (heavy right tail). Route A, training seed 0, 20 sampled tables.
